## terms explained
- resolved shear stress / разрешённое касательное напряжение: the part of the stress that acts along the slip direction in the slip plane, hence the only part that pushes a dislocation (Sec. 4, first paragraph)
- Eshelby exterior field / решение Эшелби [7]: the elastic solution for a compact particle bonded to the matrix and strained; stress falls off as (a/r)^3 (Sec. 4)
- activation volume V* / активационный объём [18]: the volume swept by a dislocation segment as it crosses an obstacle (Burgers vector times the area swept); sets how strongly the jump rate responds to stress (Sec. 4)
- thermally activated glide: dislocations pass obstacles by thermally activated jumps whose rate changes by exp(+-V*tau/kT) (Sec. 4)
- Shi(x): defined explicitly as the integral of sinh(u)/u from 0 to x, the hyperbolic sine integral (Sec. 4)
- dilute limit / Wigner-Seitz cutoff -> replaced by plain words: the integral assumes the stressed shells of neighbouring particles do not overlap; cutting it off where they would changes the required stress by 0.007% (Sec. 4)
- Schmid projection -> replaced: only a part of the interface stress acts in any one slip plane (Sec. 4, 5.1)
- bonded inclusion / сцепленное с матрицей включение: no sliding at the interface; atoms on both sides interact through the same potential and no artificial constraint is applied there (Sec. 5.1)
- eigenstrain / maintained eigenstrain / affine surrogate / imposed mode -> replaced throughout by: the imposed strain (0.194% elongation along the field direction at constant volume), the relaxed response of the cell, versus a strain held fixed
- the 5.3 MPa scale (formerly 'generous scale' 2 mu lambda_s at 100 ppm) -> stated as: the upper bound 2 mu_Al x 10^-4 = 5.3 MPa for an inclusion bonded to the matrix and strained by 10^-4, the upper end of the strain measured for iron-aluminium alloys under field [11,12]; 'deliberately favourable to the mechanism' replaces 'generous'
- ridge / гребень: a bar of half-elliptic cross section running the full length of the cell along y (Sec. 5.4, at first use in these sections)
- line tension bound mu b/L: a dislocation behaves as a line under tension; to bow it through a stressed region of size L the stress must exceed about mu b/L [18] (Sec. 5.2)
- dislocation dipole -> replaced by 'the dislocation pair' / 'a pre-existing pair of dislocations' (Sec. 5.1, 6)
- annihilation -> 'when the two dislocations meet and annihilate' (Sec. 5.4)
- seed -> 'a set of initial atomic velocities' (Sec. 5.4)
- thermostat: the algorithm that holds the cell at 300 K; NVE -> 'with the thermostat switched off after the temperature has been established' (Sec. 5.4)
- virial stresses on bins -> 'computed from the atomic forces and positions (the virial expression [8]) averaged over slabs 4 A thick parallel to the interface' (Sec. 5.4)
- spin-dependent Alshits-Molotskii channel -> 'mechanisms in which the field acts directly on the dislocation or on the obstacle, as in the theories of [1,2]'; no mention of spins (Sec. 5.4)
- dilute paramagnet / Mossbauer hyperfine splitting -> 'shows no magnetic order down to 2 K, neither in its magnetization nor in Mossbauer spectra, which probe the magnetic state of the iron atoms directly [19,20]' (Sec. 5.1)
- Joule magnetostriction / saturation -> replaced by 'change shape through magnetization'; the phrase 'ferromagnetic constituent' is used for what carries the measured hysteresis (Sec. 5.1)
- memory protocol: field on for 30 min, field removed, load applied only then; what is measured is a memory of the exposure (Sec. 5.3)
- solute strength / примесная прочность -> not used; replaced by 'the pinning by Mg and Si atoms' / 'закрепление атомами Mg и Si'
- survives / выживает / переживает -> 'remains' / 'остаётся' (Sec. 5.1, 6); 'generous' / 'щедрая' and 'not cosmetic' do not occur
