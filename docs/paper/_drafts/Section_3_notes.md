# Section 3 (Results): 3.1 The interface stress field and its decay; 3.2 Thresholds for dislocation activity at the interface; 3.3 Dislocation mobility and solute pinning in the alloy; captions of fig:sigma, fig:traj, fig:rss

## terms explained
- RSS_max — the shear stress resolved onto the slip plane and slip direction of a dislocation, taken over the twelve {111}<110> slip systems of fcc aluminium and reported as its maximum; the quantity that actually drives dislocation motion (RU: касательное напряжение, спроецированное на плоскость и направление скольжения дислокации, по всем двенадцати системам скольжения ГЦК-алюминия, приведённое по максимальной)
- von Mises stress — a single positive number that measures the shear content of a stress state and enters the yield criterion of a ductile metal (RU: напряжение фон Мизеса — единственное положительное число, характеризующее сдвиговую часть напряжённого состояния)
- bin — replaced by 'slice 4 Å thick parallel to the interface' over which the six stress components are averaged before any invariant is formed (RU: слой толщиной 4 Å, параллельный границе)
- ridge / гребень — the half-elliptic bump of the inclusion, semi-axes 35 Å along x and 20 Å along z, crest at r = 20 Å (RU: полуэллиптический выступ включения)
- coherency stress — the stress that exists because two crystals with different interatomic spacings are joined at a bonded interface: atoms on both sides are pushed away from the positions their own lattice would give them; present with or without the imposed strain (RU: напряжение когерентности границы)
- bonded interface — atoms interact across the interface through the same potential as inside either crystal, with no constraint; the interface cannot slip (RU: граница сцеплена и проскальзывать не может)
- periodic boundary — the cell is periodic along x, so a dislocation leaving through one face re-enters through the opposite one (RU: ячейка периодична вдоль x)
- dislocation dipole — a pre-existing pair of edge dislocations of opposite sign on glide planes parallel to the interface (RU: пара краевых дислокаций противоположного знака, дислокационный диполь)
- heterogeneous nucleation — creation of a dislocation at a defect, here the interface, rather than in the perfect lattice (RU: гетерогенное зарождение — образование дислокации на дефекте, а не в совершенной решётке)
- onset of dislocation motion — the stress at which the followed dislocation position departs from its thermal oscillation by more than six times the amplitude (at least 8 Å) for three consecutive frames and does not return (RU: порогом принято напряжение, при котором ...)
- Burgers vector b = 2.86 Å — the elementary displacement of the lattice produced by the passage of one dislocation (RU: вектор Бюргерса — элементарное смещение решётки при прохождении одной дислокации)
- thermostat — holds the cell at 300 K and acts on all mobile atoms, which is why the +1.4 K spike is not a calorimetric measurement (RU: термостат, удерживающий ячейку при 300 К)
- Eshelby solution — the analytical solution for an ellipsoidal (here spherical, radius 35 Å) inclusion held permanently at a given strain inside an infinite elastic matrix [Eshelby1957] (RU: аналитическое решение Эшелби для эллипсоидального включения в бесконечной упругой матрице)
- elastic estimate 2 mu_Al epsilon = 5.3 MPa — twice the shear modulus of aluminium times the strain 1.0e-4; an upper estimate of the total stress a strain of that size can produce in the matrix, taken without projection onto any slip system (replaces 'generous scale')
- solute pinning — the dislocation remains held by the dissolved Mg and Si atoms around it (RU: закрепление примесными атомами; 'примесная прочность' removed)
- Labusch estimate — the mean-field estimate of the stress needed to drag a dislocation through a random distribution of solute atoms of this concentration [Labusch1970, Leyson2010] (RU: среднеполевая оценка Лабуша)
- activation volume V* — the parameter of thermally activated glide that sets how strongly the rate of dislocation motion responds to stress (RU: активационный объём — параметр термически активированного скольжения)
- far-field level — the mean and standard deviation of RSS_max over the fifteen slices beyond 60 Å, 0.5 ± 0.6 MPa (replaces 'noise floor')
- 'survives' -> 'retains/remains'; 'relaxed response to an inflated affine surrogate' -> 'the response of the matrix to an inclusion that has been strained and then allowed to relax'; 'maintained eigenstrain' -> 'an inclusion held permanently at a given strain'

FINDING ABOUT FIG. 1 THAT THE AUTHORS MUST SEE (I did not put it in the paper; the text describes only what is plotted). To answer his questions (b) and (c) I recomputed the slice-averaged tensor from the deposited dumps (data/stageG4_clean). In the control cell above the crest the slice averages are laterally uniform with sigma_xx = sigma_yy and sigma_zz ≈ 2 sigma_xx everywhere (−596/−638/−1142 MPa at r=26; 0 at r=82; +28/+28/+56 MPa at r=118), shear components ≈ 0. The lateral average of sigma_zz (and sigma_xz) over a full periodic slice must vanish at mechanical equilibrium (no net force on the material above any plane; the ridge's own periodic field averages to zero across a slice). The logs (data/stageG4_clean/log.*) show both minimizations stopped on the ENERGY tolerance (1e-8) after 318 (control) and 234 (strained) CG iterations with final max force components 2.66 and 15.07 eV/Å (interface contacts) — the long-wavelength z-relaxation of the 158 Å slab is not converged. The 1:1:2 pattern is exactly (lambda : lambda : lambda+2mu) for a uniform z-strain with in-plane periods fixed. Consequences: (i) the whole slice-averaged top-panel profile above the crest (500 → 0 → 28 MPa) is a minimization residual as much as a coherency field, at a per-atom force level of ~1e-3 eV/Å, well inside the unconverged remainder; (ii) the 'second hump' at 46–62 Å in the bottom panel is the difference of these residuals between the two runs (Delta sigma_zz ≈ 2 Delta sigma_xx, no shear, RSS 1.5–2.3 MPa on inclined systems = 0.27 × (Delta sigma_zz − Delta sigma_xx)); (iii) the 6.3 MPa peak is the slice-averaged Delta sigma_xz, which at exact equilibrium would also average to zero — the local shear above the ridge (a laterally varying quantity) is not what the slice average measures. In the text I therefore (a) attributed the ~500 MPa AT the interface/first slice to coherency stress as instructed, (b) described the 40 Å minimum and the hump factually as 'shear on the glide plane has died out; what follows is a uniform, shear-free difference between the background stresses of the two minimized cells', (c) described the rise beyond 80 Å as 'a residual of the finite slab held at the bottom and free only at the top, uniform strain along the normal ~5e-4, identical in both cells, cancels in the difference'.
